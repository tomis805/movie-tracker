About recommendations & credits

Recommendations come from TMDB’s related-movie lists, ranked locally using your ratings: Top +3, Liked +1, Disliked −2 per matching list. Neutral and Watched have no preference weight. These scores are ranking signals, not predictions of your rating. Notes are saved for future chats but are not analyzed by this automatic ranking. Results use the first page of each related-movie list.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
